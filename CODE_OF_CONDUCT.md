# Code of conduct

We are committed to a harassment-free, technically rigorous community.
Participate in good faith, criticize ideas and implementations rather than
people, respect privacy, and do not publish vulnerability details before a
coordinated fix.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing,
and sustained disruption. Report conduct concerns privately to
`security@deliberative.io`. Maintainers may remove content or participation
that violates these expectations.
